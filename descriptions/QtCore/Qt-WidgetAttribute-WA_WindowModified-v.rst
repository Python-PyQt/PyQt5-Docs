Indicates that the window is marked as modified. On some platforms this flag will do nothing, on others (including `macOS <https://doc.qt.io/qt-5/qtwebengine-platform-notes.html#macos>`_ and Windows) the window will take a modified appearance. This flag is set or cleared by :sip:ref:`~PyQt5.QtWidgets.QWidget.setWindowModified`.

`ISO 8601 <https://doc.qt.io/qt-5/http://www.iso.org/iso/catalogue_detail?csnumber=40874>`_ extended format: either ``yyyy-MM-dd`` for dates or ``yyyy-MM-ddTHH:mm:ss`` (e.g. 2017-07-24T15:46:29), or with a time-zone suffix (Z for UTC otherwise an offset as [+|-]HH:mm) where appropriate for combined dates and times.

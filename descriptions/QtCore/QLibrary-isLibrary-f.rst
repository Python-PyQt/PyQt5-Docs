Returns ``true`` if *fileName* has a valid suffix for a loadable library; otherwise returns ``false``.

+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------+
| Platform                                                                        | Valid suffixes                   |
+=================================================================================+==================================+
| Windows                                                                         | ``.dll``, ``.DLL``               |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------+
| Unix/Linux                                                                      | ``.so``                          |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------+
| AIX                                                                             | ``.a``                           |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------+
| HP-UX                                                                           | ``.sl``, ``.so`` (HP-UXi)        |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------+
| `macOS <https://doc.qt.io/qt-5/qtwebengine-platform-notes.html#macos>`_ and iOS | ``.dylib``, ``.bundle``, ``.so`` |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------+

Trailing versioning numbers on Unix are ignored.

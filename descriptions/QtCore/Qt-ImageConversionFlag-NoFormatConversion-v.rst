Don't do any format conversions on the image. Can be useful when converting a :sip:ref:`~PyQt5.QtGui.QImage` to a :sip:ref:`~PyQt5.QtGui.QPixmap` for a one-time rendering operation for example. Note that a :sip:ref:`~PyQt5.QtGui.QPixmap` not in the preferred format will be much slower as a paint device.

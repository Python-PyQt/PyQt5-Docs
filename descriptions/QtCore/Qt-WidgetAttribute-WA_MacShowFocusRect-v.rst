Indicates that this widget should get a QFocusFrame around it. Some widgets draw their own focus halo regardless of this attribute. Not that the :sip:ref:`~PyQt5.QtWidgets.QWidget.focusPolicy` also plays the main role in whether something is given focus or not, this only controls whether or not this gets the focus frame. This attribute is only applicable to `macOS <https://doc.qt.io/qt-5/qtwebengine-platform-notes.html#macos>`_.

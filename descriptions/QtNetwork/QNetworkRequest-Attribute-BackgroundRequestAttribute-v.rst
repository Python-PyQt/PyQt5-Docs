Type: :sip:ref:`~PyQt5.QtCore.QMetaType.Type.Bool` (default: false) Indicates that this is a background transfer, rather than a user initiated transfer. Depending on the platform, background transfers may be subject to different policies. The :sip:ref:`~PyQt5.QtNetwork.QNetworkSession` ConnectInBackground property will be set according to this attribute.
